## Risks

- **`assembleDebug` may be slow or flaky on CI runners** (KMP + Compose + Android). If the run exceeds ~20 minutes, narrow the CI step to `:composeApp:assembleDebug` or a `compileDebugKotlin`-only check rather than dropping the workflow.
- **iOS targets cannot build on `ubuntu-latest`.** Keep this workflow Android/JVM-only; an iOS job needs `macos-latest` and is out of scope here.
